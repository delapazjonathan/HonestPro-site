COMMON REPAIR QUESTIONS

Symptoms worth checking

These examples can help you describe the issue when you request service in Austin. They aren't a substitute for a hands-on diagnosis.

Oven heats unevenly

Ignition, sensing and control issues may affect heating performance; diagnosis depends on the exact model and symptoms.

Refrigerator or freezer issues

No-cool conditions, ice maker failures, fan problems and frost buildup are among the concerns we troubleshoot.

Dishwasher will not complete a cycle

Drainage, door-latch, fill and pump problems can interrupt a dishwasher cycle.

Frequently asked in Austin

Austin is a primary service area, but appointment availability can vary by address. Enter your ZIP code through online booking or call us to confirm coverage for your location.
