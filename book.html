COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions before scheduling?

Here's what to know before you book a service visit.

Will I be charged the $89 diagnostic fee if I approve the repair?

No. HonestPro waives the $89 diagnostic fee when you proceed with the repair. Labor and required parts are charged for the approved repair.

Is my appointment confirmed when I click Book Online?

No. Clicking Book Online simply opens Housecall Pro. Follow the booking steps there and review the confirmation provided by Housecall Pro.

Do you offer a warranty on completed repairs?

Yes. Our standard 90-day warranty covers parts and labor for the particular issue repaired. A separate, unrelated problem is not covered under that repair's warranty.

What if I don't see a suitable time or I'm outside your listed service area?

Call us at (512) 663-2860 to discuss your location and availability. Online options may not cover every situation.
