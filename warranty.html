Ask a Warranty Question
SIMPLE, STRAIGHTFORWARD COVERAGE

We stand behind the repair we make.

What is covered?

For 90 days, our standard warranty covers both the replacement part and labor associated with the original repair. Coverage is limited to the specific problem and repair we completed.

What is considered a separate issue?

A different appliance problem that develops later is not part of the original repair warranty and would be diagnosed and charged separately.

Need help with the original repair?

Contact us with your name, appliance details, and original service date. We’ll review the repair and your warranty question.

A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Same repair vs. a new problem.

If we replace your refrigerator’s evaporator fan motor, the standard warranty applies to that fan-motor repair for 90 days, including the installed part and labor.

If the ice maker stops working a month later because of an unrelated issue, that is a separate repair and would be charged accordingly.

The warranty is specific to the repair we performed, not every component in the appliance.
