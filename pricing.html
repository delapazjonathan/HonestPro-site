COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked pricing questions.

When do I pay the $89 diagnostic fee?

The $89 fee covers diagnosing the appliance. It is waived when you proceed with the repair, so you pay for parts and the flat-rate labor instead. If you decline the repair, the $89 diagnostic fee applies.

How much do you charge for labor?

Labor is charged at a flat rate of $110–$210 depending on the repair and its complexity. Parts are charged separately, and the $89 diagnostic fee is waived when you proceed with the repair.

Can you give an exact price over the phone?

We can discuss symptoms and likely next steps, but a reliable repair quote typically requires diagnosing the appliance and identifying the correct parts.

Should I repair or replace my appliance?

We can help you weigh the repair cost and the appliance's age and condition so you can choose what makes sense.
