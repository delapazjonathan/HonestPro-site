COMMON REPAIR QUESTIONS

Symptoms worth checking

These examples can help you describe the issue when you request service in Lockhart. They aren't a substitute for a hands-on diagnosis.

Oven will not bake or broil

Igniter, heating, temperature-sensing and control issues can affect cooking performance.

Electric cooktop or burner problems

A burner that stays cold or heats inconsistently may require electrical and component testing.

Refrigerator is not cooling

Airflow, temperature controls and other components can cause loss of cooling and should be diagnosed before replacing parts.

BEFORE YOUR APPOINTMENT

Before scheduling range or oven service in Lockhart

Keep the model number handy and describe whether the issue affects bake, broil, the cooktop or more than one function. If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services rather than operating the appliance.

Have your brand and model number available when possible, along with a short description of the symptoms. Your exact address helps confirm availability.
