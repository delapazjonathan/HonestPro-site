About HonestPro

Experienced appliance repair with honest recommendations and straightforward service.

HonestPro Appliance Repair was built around a simple idea: give homeowners reliable appliance repair without the runaround.

With 10+ years of hands-on experience, HonestPro provides professional service for major household appliances, including many higher-end units. The focus is always on accurate diagnosis, clear communication, and repairs that make sense for the customer.

Whether the issue is with a refrigerator, washer, dryer, oven, dishwasher, or another major appliance, the goal is the same — diagnose the problem correctly, explain the repair clearly, and help you make the right decision for your home.

What customers can expect

  • Honest guidance on repair versus replacement
  • Professional, respectful in-home service
  • Clear explanation of the problem and repair options
  • Experience with major brands and many high-end appliances

What sets the business apart

Accurate diagnosis

Good repair starts with identifying the real cause of the problem, not guessing or replacing parts unnecessarily.

Honest recommendations

Some appliances are worth repairing and some are not. HonestPro is built around giving customers practical recommendations they can trust.

Dependable service

Customers should know what to expect, when to expect it, and feel confident they are working with someone who respects their home and their time.

Local service you can feel good about calling

HonestPro Appliance Repair serves homeowners in Buda, Kyle, South Austin, San Marcos, and surrounding areas with a focus on dependable repairs and straightforward communication.
